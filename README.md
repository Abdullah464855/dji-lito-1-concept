# DJI Lito 1 — Concept Landing Page

Day 2 of a 30-day drone landing page series: one drone, one landing page, every day.

A concept redesign of a product landing page for the **DJI Lito 1** beginner camera drone — "Flagship safety. Beginner price." Built as a single self-contained static HTML file (all images inlined, no build step).

## What's on the page
- Hero with ghost "LITO 1" wordmark and background-removed drone cutouts on black
- Stat row (48MP · 36 min · approx. 249 g), safety section with 360° sensing, camera section (1/2-inch 48MP, 4K/60fps HDR)
- "Shoot cinematic 4K footage" statement section, gallery bento, grouped full specifications
- Pick your kit: Standard £299 / Fly More Combo £429 (UK guide prices, vary by region)
- FAQ including the honest notes: microSD card required (no internal storage), Plus battery not supported in the EU/UK, 8 km CE vs 15 km FCC transmission range
- Demo checkout pop-up

## Demo checkout
The checkout is a **design demo only** — there is no payment gateway, nothing is sent or stored, and no order is placed. It exists to demonstrate the page's conversion flow.

## Fonts
Open Sans (body/UI) and DM Sans ExtraBold (headlines) — the DJI Store pairing; DJI's own display font (DJI Sans) is proprietary, and DM Sans is the closest free match.
